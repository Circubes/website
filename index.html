How it works

Snap. Circuit. Aha.

Circubes® turns invisible electricity into something kids can see, hear, and rearrange in seconds.

2

Learning moments

From first light to real systems

Each build teaches a concept you can feel — then expand into something bigger.

Learns: sensing & polarity

Light-reactive circuit

Cover the photoresistor and watch the LED respond. Kids discover sensors, current direction, and cause-and-effect in one satisfying moment.

Learns: energy & motion

Motor + LED demo

Battery power becomes spin and light — a tangible bridge from abstract “energy” to something spinning on the table.

Learns: components & symbols

Read the language of circuits

Schematic symbols and values printed on every cube make real electronics vocabulary stick through play.
