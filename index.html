What is Circubes?

A hands-on electronics learning kit made of magnetic cubes. Each cube contains a real electronic component that snaps together to form complete working circuits.

Designed for learners of any age or educational background, Circubes removes the complexity of wires, breadboards, and soldering, making electronics education safe, intuitive, and fun.

Why Circubes is Different
